Energy and loads

The numbers above the graphs are the events in the table. Air drag on the machine at 100% uses the arm and counterweight sizes from the CAD, and a sling value tuned so the throw lands at the measured 115 ft. The counterweight mass slider changes the mass only; its moment of inertia about its own centre stays at the CAD value. Peak loads are up to the moment of release. Angles are measured from horizontal, and the arm turns clockwise, so they count down.
